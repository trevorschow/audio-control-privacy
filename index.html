Privacy Policy — Audio Control

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Audio Control controls your multi-zone home audio amplifier over your own local network. That's all it does, and that's the whole privacy story.

No data collection

The app collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

On-device only

Your settings — zone names, network configuration, groups, favorites, and preferences — are stored only on your phone. They never leave your device.

Local network only

The app communicates with your amplifier or network adapter on your home LAN. Nothing is sent to the internet, to the developer, or to any third party.
